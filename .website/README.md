**Narwhals of the Rainbow Sea** is a colorful cooperative adventure.
Swim through seas of every color, discover friends who need your help, and guide them back to the sea where they
belong.
_Friendship is magic_: stick together, look out for one another, and
work as a team to reach the depths and overcome whatever lurks there.

Supports **1–2 local players**. Player 2 can join at any time by pressing the
**2P** button.

### Controls 🎮 🎮

- **Player 1:** Arrow keys to swim, **Space** to boost
- **Player 2:** **WASD** to swim, **E** to boost
- **Gamepad:** Left stick to swim, **X / Square** to boost
- **Touch:** Drag the on-screen control; press the ⚡ button to boost

![Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scmx/js13k2026/refs/heads/main/screenshot.png)

### Online

While playing you can see other players that are currently playing as smaller faint fishes in the background. You cannot interact with them, but I thought it would be fun to be able to see if there are other players playing the game currently.
